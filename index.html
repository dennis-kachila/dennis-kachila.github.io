Frequently Asked Questions

What electrical engineering services do you offer in Nairobi?

I provide MEP electrical design, domestic and industrial wiring installation, electrical maintenance support, MATLAB/Simulink modelling, and 3D CAD design using SolidWorks and Fusion 360.

Do you handle both domestic and industrial electrical projects?

Yes. I work on both domestic and industrial projects, including wiring layouts, load distribution, installation planning, and testing support based on project requirements.

Can you help with MATLAB and Simulink power system modelling?

Yes. I develop and analyze power system models in MATLAB and Simulink for studies such as load flow, fault response, machine behaviour, and performance optimization.

Do you offer CAD design services for engineering projects?

Yes. I offer CAD modelling and design services in Revit, AutoCAD, SolidWorks, and Fusion 360 for engineering visualization, documentation, and implementation planning.
